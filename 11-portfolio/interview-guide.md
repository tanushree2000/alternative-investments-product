# Interview Guide

## 60-second story
“I wanted to understand product management inside alternative asset management, so I built a case study around a problem that connects deal manufacturing and portfolio management. I mapped the investment lifecycle and reviewed public private-markets products. My hypothesis was that the assumptions used to approve an investment can lose context when monitoring moves into a separate workflow. I designed Northstar Thesis Monitor, which freezes approved assumptions, maps them to actual KPIs and surfaces material exceptions. I then carried the concept through prioritization, metrics, synthetic analytics, financial modeling, architecture, vendor strategy, Jira-ready delivery and UAT. The targets are modeled, not real production results.”

## Likely questions
**Why not build another dashboard?** Because data aggregation is already common; the differentiated user decision is interpreting actual performance against the approved case.

**Why not use AI?** The MVP first needs governed baselines, mappings and auditability. AI can summarize later, but should not replace investment judgment.

**What is IRR?** Annualized return measure considering timing of cash flows.

**What is MOIC?** Total value received/current value divided by invested capital; unlike IRR, it does not capture time.

**Biggest product risk?** Standardizing thesis metrics across investment strategies and maintaining trustworthy source mappings.

**Build vs buy?** Buy market/reference data; build the thesis-to-exception workflow because it is the core differentiated product logic.
