# Product Charter

## Product
**Northstar Thesis Monitor**

## Vision
Give alternative-investment teams a continuous line of sight from the assumptions used to deploy capital to the actual performance of the investment.

## Problem
Approved investment assumptions and post-investment performance can live in different workflows, making exceptions harder to interpret and act on.

## Users
Portfolio Manager, Investment Associate, Investment Committee, Investment Operations/Data.

## Value proposition
Northstar converts approved thesis assumptions into monitored benchmarks, surfaces material variance, and creates an accountable investigation workflow.

## In scope
Deal workspace, IC approval snapshot, thesis metric mapping, actual-vs-plan monitoring, exception rules, ownership, comments, audit trail.

## Out of scope
Fraud detection, accounting ledger, autonomous investing, market-data creation, transaction execution.
