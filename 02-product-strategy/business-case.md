# Business Case

## Business hypothesis
Northstar can reduce manual portfolio-review preparation and improve consistency of exception detection.

## Illustrative baseline
Assume 20 investment professionals each spend 6 hours/month preparing recurring portfolio review materials.

Annual effort = 20 × 6 × 12 = **1,440 hours**.

At an illustrative loaded cost of $120/hour, annual effort value = **$172,800**.

A 25% reduction target would free approximately **360 hours/year**, or **$43,200** of modeled capacity.

## Important
This is a **modeled business case**, not an achieved saving. It excludes implementation cost, vendor cost, change-management cost and benefits from earlier risk identification.

## Decision
Proceed to MVP if discovery validates thesis continuity as a recurring pain and integration complexity is manageable.
