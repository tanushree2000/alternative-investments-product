# MVP Scope

## Must have
- Deal and investment record
- Approved thesis baseline
- KPI/milestone mapping
- Actual KPI ingestion via CSV/API
- Variance calculation
- Configurable thresholds
- Exception queue
- Owner/status/comments
- Audit history
- Portfolio summary

## Should have
- IC snapshot export
- Role-based access
- reminder notifications
- basic vendor/data-source health

## Could have
- scenario overlays
- benchmark context
- narrative summaries

## Won't have in MVP
- AI investment recommendations
- full fund accounting
- CRM replacement
- automated valuation
