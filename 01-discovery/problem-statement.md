# Problem Statement

## Problem
Investment teams can approve a deal using a detailed thesis, return model, risks and strategic milestones, then monitor the investment later through a separate reporting process. When those contexts are not explicitly linked, teams may spend additional time reconstructing why an investment was approved and identifying which deviations are material.

## How might we
**How might we preserve the assumptions behind an investment decision and convert them into measurable monitoring benchmarks so portfolio teams can identify and investigate material deviations earlier?**

## Target user
Primary: Portfolio Manager / Investment Lead  
Secondary: Investment Associate, IC member, Operations/Data

## Non-goals
Northstar will not make autonomous investment decisions, replace fund accounting, replace CRM, execute trades, or predict returns with AI.
