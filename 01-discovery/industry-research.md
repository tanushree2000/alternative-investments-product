# Industry Research

## Objective
Understand the private-markets workflow from opportunity identification through portfolio monitoring and identify where product technology can improve decision quality and operating scalability.

## Public-market context
Alternative investment technology spans deal tracking, diligence, portfolio monitoring, performance/risk analysis, data management, reporting, and workflow automation. This case study uses public product descriptions from BlackRock Aladdin/eFront, Allvue, Preqin, and general private-markets research as context.

## Simplified lifecycle
1. Deal sourcing
2. Initial screening
3. Due diligence
4. Financial modeling and valuation
5. Investment committee review
6. Deal execution
7. Portfolio monitoring
8. Value creation
9. Exit

## Research observation
A recurring product opportunity exists at the transition between underwriting and portfolio management: the assumptions used to approve a deal should remain visible after investment so actual performance can be compared with the original case.

## Evidence / assumption discipline
- **Evidence:** public private-markets platforms support deal, portfolio, analytics and reporting workflows.
- **Hypothesis:** investment-thesis context can become fragmented across lifecycle stages.
- **Portfolio decision:** Northstar focuses on thesis continuity rather than replacing every investment system.
