# Competitive Analysis

## Market categories
Private-markets technology includes investment CRM/deal management, diligence workflow, portfolio monitoring, fund accounting, investor reporting, market intelligence and analytics.

| Capability | Deal/CRM tools | Portfolio tools | Enterprise alternatives suites | Northstar focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deal pipeline | Strong | Limited | Strong | Included |
| Diligence workflow | Strong | Limited | Strong | Included |
| IC workflow | Moderate | Limited | Varies | Included |
| Portfolio KPI monitoring | Limited | Strong | Strong | Included |
| Expected vs actual | Varies | Strong financially | Strong | Core |
| Thesis-to-KPI continuity | Varies | Varies | Varies | Core |
| Exception workflow | Varies | Varies | Varies | Core |

## Strategic conclusion
“Single source of truth” is not sufficient differentiation. Northstar differentiates through **decision context preservation**: approved assumptions become measurable post-investment benchmarks.

## Competitive caution
This matrix is a portfolio hypothesis based on public product descriptions. It does not claim competitors are technically incapable of the proposed workflow.
