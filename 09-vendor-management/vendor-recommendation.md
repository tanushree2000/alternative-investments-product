# Vendor Recommendation

Use a third-party provider for market/reference data and retain Northstar ownership of thesis mapping, variance logic and exception workflow.

## Why
Buying data accelerates coverage; building the decision workflow preserves differentiation and avoids making a vendor the system of product logic.

## Contract focus
API stability, historical coverage, entity identifiers, data freshness, security, support, roadmap transparency and termination/data-portability terms.
