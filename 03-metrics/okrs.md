# OKRs

## Objective 1: Make approved investment assumptions operational
- KR1: 70% of active investments have governed thesis baselines within 90 days of rollout.
- KR2: 90% of required thesis KPIs are mapped to a validated data source.
- KR3: 95% of baseline changes retain approver and audit history.

## Objective 2: Make portfolio exceptions actionable
- KR1: 90% of material rule-based variances surface within one reporting cycle.
- KR2: 80% of P0/P1 exceptions receive an owner within 2 business days.
- KR3: false escalation rate remains below 2%.

These are **product targets**, not achieved results.
