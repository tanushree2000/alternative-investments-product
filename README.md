# Northstar Alternative Investments: Deal & Portfolio Management Platform

**Portfolio case study | Product Management | Private Markets**

Northstar is a decision-support and workflow platform for an alternative asset manager. The case study explores a product opportunity connecting **deal underwriting and investment-committee decisions with post-investment portfolio monitoring**.


## Product thesis

Private-market teams make investment decisions using an investment thesis, financial assumptions, diligence findings, risks, and strategic milestones. After capital is deployed, those assumptions can become disconnected from ongoing portfolio reporting.

Northstar's core capability, **Thesis Monitor**, preserves approved assumptions as monitoring benchmarks and highlights material variance between the investment case and actual performance.

**Decision supported:** *Where has reality materially diverged from the assumptions behind our investment decision?*

## Modeled outcome targets
These are **targets, not achieved production results**:
- ≥70% of active investments mapped to approved thesis metrics within 90 days
- ≥90% of material KPI variances surfaced within one reporting cycle
- 25% reduction target in manual preparation time for recurring portfolio reviews
- <2% false escalation target for rule-based thesis exceptions
