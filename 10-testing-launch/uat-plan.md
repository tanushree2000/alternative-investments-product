# UAT Plan

## Goal
Confirm that users can create an approved thesis baseline, ingest actual performance, detect a material variance, assign an exception and close it with a traceable rationale.

## Participants
Investment Associate, Portfolio Manager, Operations/Data, Product, QA.

## Entry criteria
P0/P1 engineering defects closed; test data loaded; roles configured; integrations stable.

## Exit criteria
100% critical scenarios pass; no open Sev-1/Sev-2 defects; reconciliation complete; rollback tested; business owner signs off.
